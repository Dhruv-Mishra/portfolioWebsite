Relaxed Concurrent Counting Bloom Filters, my undergraduate research at IIIT Delhi's Distributed Computing Lab under Prof. Bapi Chatterjee. I built a lock-free C++ Counting Bloom Filter that uses relaxed synchronization to get 3x the throughput of the baseline under high contention, with little effect on false positive and false negative rates. It was benchmarked against existing concurrent filter implementations and published in the IIIT Delhi repository. Stack: C++, Bloom Filters, Concurrency, Optimization, Data Structures. Year: 2024, 8 months. Link: repository.iiitd.edu.in/jspui/handle/123456789/1613.
