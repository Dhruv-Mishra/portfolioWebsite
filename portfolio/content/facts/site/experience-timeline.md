The about page (/about) includes an interactive experience timeline populated with Dhruv's real career data. It covers his current Software Engineer role at Microsoft, where he builds production AI agents and the Azure backend services they run on; his Machine Learning Engineer role at growIndigo; his Microsoft Loop software engineering internship; IIIT Delhi CSAM education; DCLL research; and competitive programming / award highlights. Each entry is an instant photo clipped to a wire mesh with a binder clip (or pegged to a twine line, in Settings > Customization) with the logo and role in the picture and the dates written underneath. Hovering a photo slides out a one-line impact summary; opening one unclips it and flies it to the middle of the screen with the highlights, location, dates, and tools.
