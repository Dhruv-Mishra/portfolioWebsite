Site pages: home (/), projects (/projects), about (/about), and chat (/chat) in the top tabs, plus the guestbook (/guestbook) and the sticker album (/stickers). The home page pairs a short intro and a Talk to AI Dhruv note (which starts a voice call with the AI version of Dhruv on the current page) with the retro terminal, a cycling slideshow of featured projects, and a short about-me card. The projects page pins every project as a polaroid on a lined sheet; opening one shows its video, details, and links. The about page has Dhruv's note with Download resume (PDF) and Open in a new tab buttons, his contact links, and an S-shaped experience timeline whose tiles preview on hover and stay open on click. The old /resume address now opens the about page. The chat page hosts the full AI chat, the guestbook lets visitors sign the wall, and the sticker album tracks unlocked stickers.
