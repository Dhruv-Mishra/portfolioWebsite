Site pages: home (/), projects (/projects), about (/about), and chat (/chat) in the top tabs, plus the guestbook (/guestbook) and the sticker album (/stickers). The home page pairs a short intro, a Talk to AI Dhruv note (which starts a voice call with the AI version of Dhruv on the current page), and Chat and Sign my sketchbook notes with the retro terminal, a cycling slideshow of featured projects, and a short about-me card. The projects page tapes a paper card for each project to the page: a preview still from its sketchbook video, the project's kind and languages, a few highlighted lines of its story, its stack, and links; opening a card peels its tape and flies it out into a full note with its video, details, and links. The about page is a cork pin board of notes: about me, a photo, the resume with Download PDF and Open in a new tab links, contact details, what Dhruv is working on now, his hobbies, and a Talk to AI Dhruv note. Opening a note pulls its pin and flies it to the middle of the screen; closing pins it back. Below the board, the experience timeline hangs as instant photos clipped to a wire mesh, newest first: each photo develops as it scrolls in, shows the role, logo, and dates, slides out a one-line summary on hover, and unclips and flies open into the full story on click. The old /resume address now opens the about page. The chat page hosts the full AI chat, the guestbook lets visitors sign the wall, and the sticker album tracks unlocked stickers.
