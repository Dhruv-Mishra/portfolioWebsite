Site pages: home (/), projects (/projects), about (/about), and chat (/chat) in the top tabs, plus the guestbook (/guestbook) and the sticker album (/stickers). The home page pairs a short intro and a Talk to AI Dhruv note (which starts a voice call with the AI version of Dhruv on the current page) with the retro terminal (with Chat and Sign my sketchbook notes under it), a cycling slideshow of featured projects, and a short about-me card. The projects page tapes a paper card for each project to the page: a big preview photo, the project's kind and languages, a few highlighted lines of its story, its stack, and links; opening a card flies it out into a full note with its video, details, and links. The about page is a cork pin board of notes: about me, a photo, the resume with Download PDF and Open in a new tab links, contact details, what Dhruv is working on now, his hobbies, and a Talk to AI Dhruv note. Opening a note pulls its pin and flies it to the middle of the screen; closing pins it back. Below the board, an S-shaped experience timeline shows a short preview on hover and the full card on click. The old /resume address now opens the about page. The chat page hosts the full AI chat, the guestbook lets visitors sign the wall, and the sticker album tracks unlocked stickers.
