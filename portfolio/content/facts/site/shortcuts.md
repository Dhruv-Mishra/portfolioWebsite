Keyboard shortcuts: navigation uses `g`-chord sequences — g then h for home, g p for projects, g a for about, g r for resume, g c for chat, g b for guestbook, g s for stickers. The chord window is 1.2 seconds. Actions: `t` toggles the theme, `?` opens the shortcuts overlay on desktop, `Esc` closes overlays. Shortcuts do not intercept typing in editable fields or system modifier keys.
