The site has 11 regular unlockable stickers earned by exploring, plus a hidden completion award ("Superuser") for completionists who collect all 11. Every sticker is reachable on touch devices — nothing is keyboard-only. The 11 regular stickers in STICKER_ROSTER are: "The First Word" (type your first terminal command), "Lights On, Lights Off" (toggle the theme between day and night), "Pen Pal" (send a note via feedback), "The Whole Tour" (visit every page on the site), "Serious Chat" (have a real chat on the chat page), "Left a Mark" (pin a note to the guestbook), "Case Files" (open a project note on /projects), "Chat Conductor" (ask the AI chat to perform an action), "Repo Hunter" (follow a project back to its source repo), "Social Butterfly" (tap one of the social sidebar links), and "Phoned a Friend" (call the website voice agent). Browse unlocked stickers at /stickers. Hardcore collectors who collect all 11 regular stickers automatically unlock the hidden completion award: "Superuser".
