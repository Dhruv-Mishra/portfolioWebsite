The home terminal accepts real commands: help, about, projects, contact, socials, ls, cat [file], open [file], clear, joke, skills, resume, cv, chat, feedback, guestbook, sign, stickers, cheatsheet, sudo [cmd], init, whoami, date, github, and linkedin. `ls` lists fake files such as about.md, projects.json, cropio.md, resume.pdf, contact.txt, and secrets.env. `cat` reads available files, `open resume.pdf` opens the resume page, and `joke` uses JokeAPI.

Site interactions have lightweight, procedurally synthesized Web Audio sounds for page turns, chat, stickers, themes, terminal input, and modals. A sitewide speaker toggle mutes those sounds plus disco and Matrix music. It respects browser autoplay rules and quiets when the tab is hidden.

`sudo` commands are denied until the visitor earns every sticker. After root unlock, `sudo help` lists hidden commands and `sudo cheatsheet` shows sticker progress. Hidden commands include `sudo disco`, `sudo matrix`, `sudo rainbow`, `sudo fortune`, `sudo whoami`, and destructive `sudo reset yes`. The exact unlock trigger remains for visitors to discover.

`sudo disco` and `sudo matrix` require a second confirmation with `yes`; `no` aborts. Matrix mode persists across navigation and refresh, and only its on-screen `WAKE UP` or later escape button can dismiss it. Disco exits through `sudo disco off` or the theme toggle.
