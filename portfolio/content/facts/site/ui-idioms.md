The site's visual idiom is a hand-drawn sketchbook: paper textures, torn-tape strips, thumbtacks, ruled-line backgrounds, folded-corner notes, a custom pencil/chalk cursor that leaves a short trail, the handwritten Patrick Hand face, and Fira Code for terminal and code. Paragraphs default to the handwritten face; Settings > Reading font switches them to Clean (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next) for easier reading while headings and notes stay handwritten. Everything supports light and dark themes: light mode is a sketchbook page and dark mode is a chalkboard with chalk doodles, while notes stay real paper. Settings > Look and feel switches the light page between drawing paper, painter's canvas, and graph paper; the dark page between a black chalkboard, a green chalkboard, and flat slate; notes between textured paper, kraft cardboard, and flat sticky notes, with or without torn edges; the about page between a cork or burlap pin board and notes laid on the page; how a note opens (pulling its pin, a zoom, or a fade); how many doodles, origami, and props decorate the margins; and how much wear (coffee rings, foxing, ghosted chalk) shows.
