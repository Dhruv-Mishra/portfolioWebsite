The site's visual idiom is a hand-drawn sketchbook: paper textures, torn-tape strips, thumbtacks, ruled-line backgrounds, folded-corner notes, a custom pencil/chalk cursor that leaves a short trail, the handwritten Patrick Hand face, and Fira Code for terminal and code. Paragraphs default to the handwritten face; Settings > Reading font switches them to Clean (Atkinson Hyperlegible Next) for easier reading while headings and notes stay handwritten. Everything supports light and dark themes: light mode is a sketchbook page and dark mode is a chalkboard with chalk doodles, while notes stay real paper. The Settings > Customization drawer holds the paper stock (four colour sheets), paper grain, tape, sketch outlines, page flips, motion, and the look choices: it switches the light page between painter's canvas (the default), drawing paper, and graph paper; the dark page between a black chalkboard, a green chalkboard, and flat slate; notes between textured paper, kraft cardboard, and flat sticky notes, with or without torn edges; project cards between kraft cardboard (the default) and the notes' material; the about page between a cork or burlap pin board and notes laid on the page; the experience photos between a wire mesh with binder clips (the default) and a twine line with wooden pegs; how a note opens (unfastening it first by pulling the pin, peeling the tape, or opening the peg, a plain zoom, or a fade); how many line-sketch doodles and chalk drawings decorate the margins (lots, by default); and how much wear (a faint coffee ring, foxing when well-worn, ghosted chalk) shows. The site opens in dark mode on the green chalkboard by default.
